Jan Mateen,

Main jaanta hon ke maine tumhara dil dukhaya, aur ye baat mujhe andar tak khal rahi hai. Meri galti thi — main us waqt tumhari baat samajh nahi saka, apni zid mein tha. Us waqt ka mera ek lafz aaj bhi mujhe yaad aata hai, aur mujhe sharmindagi hoti hai.

Tum sirf ek dost nahi ho. Tum wo shakhs ho jise main jan kehta hon — kyunke tum meri zindagi ka wo hissa ho jo main khona nahi chahta, na aaj, na kabhi. Humari dosti mein jo bhi kami aayi, us ka sab se bara hissa meri hi thi.

Main tumse ye nahi keh raha ke tum bhool jao. Main ye keh raha hon ke mujhe maaf kar do — taake hum phir se wo log ban sakein jo hum the.

Main tumse muhabbat karta hon, Mateen. Ye wo muhabbat nahi jo aadat ban jaye — ye wo hai jo waqt ke saath badalti nahi, sirf gehra hoti jati hai. Tum mere liye ahem ho, aur hamesha rahoge.

Agar tum mujhe ek mauqa do, main tumhein sabit kar dunga ke main badal gaya hon. Bas itna — ek baar baat kar lo mujhse. Baqi main dekh lunga.
